08 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology with a purpose.

ECOAI is an academic project concept exploring how Artificial Intelligence can support environmental awareness, data interpretation and sustainable decision-making.

The platform demonstrates concepts such as computer vision, data visualisation, conversational systems and sustainability analytics in one unified experience.

5TH SEMESTER · VTUARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Make environmental information easier to understand.

Demonstrate AI concepts for environmental assessment.

Encourage sustainable everyday decisions.

04 · Interactive learning

Use visual tools to make sustainability engaging.
